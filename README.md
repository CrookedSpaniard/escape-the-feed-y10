# Escape the Feed · Year 10 Online Safety

An escape room for teams of 2–3 players (30–35 minutes) for the British School Alzira · Xàtiva · Gandia Group.

- Four zones, one per lesson: Threat Arcade (being safe online), Reputation Road (online reputation and digital footprint), The Data Mine (big data and data protection), Privacy Fortress (right to privacy).
- 12 challenges (match, sort, spot the posts) and 4 junctions with trap doors. Each zone gives a key; four keys open the Exit Vault, followed by a turn-based final showdown.
- Hints, “Help us through”, points, stars, certificate and a copyable results report. Progress is saved on the device only.

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Content lives in `js/data.js`. The teacher solutions guide is kept outside this folder so it is never published.
